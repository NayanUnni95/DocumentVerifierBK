How It Works

Secure verification in three simple steps.

1. Upload

Institutions upload certificates using our secure dashboard. AI instantly extracts the critical data.

2. Anchor

A unique cryptographic fingerprint (hash) is minted specifically to the Polygon blockchain.

3. Verify

Graduates or employers verify authenticity in seconds using the Document ID or file.
